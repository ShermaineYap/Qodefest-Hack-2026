The problem

95% of online fraud is authorised by the victim.

95%

of cases involve the victim approving the transfer themselves. The stolen credential is not the attack.

28%

of total scam losses fall on older adults — under 10% of victims.

RM1.47B

lost to investment scams alone, coordinated mainly through chat apps.

hours

a new lookalike domain can be live and convincing before any blocklist catches it.

The link is the delivery mechanism. The chat carries the pressure; the link carries the theft. That conversation is visible immediately — and a plain blocklist throws it away.
Why not just Safe Browsing

Keep the blocklist. Add the context it cannot see.

Blocklist alone necessary, late

  • cannot explain why a link is bad
  • ignores the conversation that delivered it
  • arrives after a fresh domain already worked
  • red pages train people to click through

Lantern context + consequence

  • a blocklist hit stays a hard HIGH
  • reads the link where it arrived
  • says “it asks for your bank login, but it is not your bank”
  • slows the decision once, then gets out of the way

A clean link gets a badge too. An indicator that only appears on danger is indistinguishable from one that is broken.

The product

Two surfaces, one detection pipeline.

Passive

Inline badges

Finds links in the conversation already on screen and marks each one in place — 🟢 low, 🟡 medium, 🔴 high, ⋯ checking, ⚠ not checked.

Interrupt once

Warning page

A HIGH top-level navigation is replaced by a calm page that names the consequence. “Go back to safety” is the default and the dominant action.

Active

Assistant panel

Paste a link or a whole message. The panel answers with evidence, shows what was checked and what could not be, and recommends one next step.

The guard is passive: no button to press. The assistant is active: for the moment she wants a second opinion.

Design rules
  • back is filled; continue is plain text
  • no countdown, no pre-selection
  • consequence, not mechanics: “not your bank”
  • HIGH interrupts once; MEDIUM only annotates

Trust boundary

What leaves the device is not the URL.

Leaves the device

  • registrable domain + TLD
  • coarse path shape — /login, /claim
  • structural flags — IP literal, subdomain count, punycode
  • hashed session id
  • tier, score, pattern names, request id

Never leaves the device

  • full URL with query string
  • page text, chat text, cookies
  • email, phone, NRIC, card, account, wallet
  • contact names, thread titles
  • browsing history, click history, verdict log
Nothing is written to disk. A record of what has been trying to scam her is useful to exactly one other party.
Non-negotiable rules

If Lantern cannot check, it says so.

NOT CHECKED is never LOW

Backend unreachable, timeout, unreadable answer — all become “could not check”. No verdict is invented.

The worst verdict wins

The newest answer never walks a HIGH back to green in the seconds that matter.

No model may lower a floor

Models supply evidence. Code owns the threshold. A failed model drops out; it is not a vote for LOW.

No policy id, no scoring

Every verdict is filed against a policy, so a swapped session id cannot launder a HIGH away.

Selector drift is treated the same way: a broken read is reported as NOT CHECKED, not shown as green.
